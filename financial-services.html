Financial Services / Integration

Payment System Integration

Secure payment gateway integration, Open Banking API connectivity, and PCI DSS-aligned transaction handling built for production from day one.

About This Service

Quatech integrates payment infrastructure into web applications and platforms with security as the primary design constraint. We work with Stripe, Open Banking APIs, and other payment gateways — handling authentication, webhook verification, idempotency, error handling, and reconciliation correctly from the start rather than retrofitting security onto a working but insecure implementation.

Our DevSecOps background means payment integrations are built with end-to-end TLS, server-side validation of all payment events, no client-side trust for amounts or authorisations, and webhook signature verification throughout. Sensitive card data never touches your server — tokenisation is used wherever the payment provider supports it.

PCI DSS scope reduction is a core consideration in every payment integration design: the goal is to minimise your compliance obligations through architecture, not just pass an audit.

Stripe payment integration (Checkout, PaymentIntents, Webhooks)

Open Banking API connectivity (account info, payment initiation)

Webhook signature verification and idempotency handling

PCI DSS scope reduction through tokenisation architecture

Transaction reconciliation and reporting pipelines

Sandbox testing against provider test environments before go-live

Integration Process

01

Payment Requirements & Scoping

We document payment flows, currencies, subscription vs one-off, refund handling, and compliance obligations. PCI DSS scope is assessed and a scope-reduction architecture agreed.

02

Provider Selection & Account Setup

Payment provider is confirmed or recommended. Sandbox accounts are configured and webhook endpoints are set up for development testing.

03

Secure Integration Development

Server-side payment handling is built: PaymentIntent creation, webhook verification, idempotency keys, and error handling. Client-side Elements or Checkout used to avoid touching raw card data.

04

Testing & Edge Case Validation

Full payment flow is tested: successful payments, card declines, insufficient funds, fraud blocks, webhook retries, and refund flows. All validated in sandbox before live credentials are issued.

05

Reconciliation & Reporting

Transaction data is reconciled against provider records. Reporting pipeline is built for finance team access to transaction history, failed payments, and subscription status.

06

Go-Live & Monitoring

Live credentials are configured in environment variables, payment monitoring is set up, and a 30-day post-launch support window covers any production issues.

Financial Services / Compliance

RegTech & Compliance

AML/KYC process design, GDPR advisory, FCA regulatory guidance, and compliance automation — making regulatory obligations manageable and auditable.

About This Service

Regulatory compliance in financial services is not optional — but it can be handled intelligently. Quatech helps financial businesses design and implement AML/KYC workflows that are both compliant and operationally efficient, GDPR-aligned data handling practices that are practical rather than paper-based, and FCA regulatory frameworks that are understood and embedded rather than filed and forgotten.

Where regulatory processes can be automated — identity verification workflows, transaction monitoring rules, audit log generation, compliance reporting dashboards — we build the tooling. Where human judgement is required, we design the processes and escalation paths that support it.

Our approach is to understand what a regulator is actually trying to achieve, then design systems that genuinely meet that intent rather than just ticking boxes that could be challenged on audit.

AML customer risk scoring and transaction monitoring design

KYC/KYB workflow design and identity verification integration

GDPR data mapping, retention schedules, and privacy notices

FCA regulatory framework advisory for authorised/exempt businesses

Compliance automation: audit logs, SAR workflows, regulatory reporting

Pre-audit preparation and compliance gap analysis

Service Process

01

Regulatory Scope Assessment

We identify which regulations apply to your business activities, products, and customer base: FCA permissions, AML requirements under the Money Laundering Regulations, GDPR obligations, and PCI DSS scope if relevant.

02

Gap Analysis

Current practices are assessed against regulatory requirements. Gaps are documented, risk-rated, and prioritised for remediation. The output is a clear action plan, not a list of problems.

03

Policy & Process Design

Compliant policies and operational processes are designed for AML/KYC, data protection, complaints, and other applicable areas — practical documents that staff can actually follow.

04

Technology & Automation

Where automation adds value — identity verification integration, transaction monitoring rules, compliance dashboards, audit trail logging — we build the tooling to support the process.

05

Testing & Evidence

Processes are tested against representative scenarios. Evidence of compliance (documented decisions, audit logs, training records) is structured to withstand regulatory scrutiny.

06

Ongoing Monitoring

Regulatory change monitoring and periodic compliance reviews are available as a retained service. Alerts are issued when material regulatory changes affect your obligations.

Financial Services / Security

Cybersecurity for Financial Services

Threat modelling, penetration testing, and security architecture tailored to the financial sector — where data is high-value and the consequences of a breach extend well beyond technical remediation.

About This Service

Financial services organisations face a disproportionate share of targeted cyberattacks. Payment systems, customer PII, account credentials, and financial transaction data are among the most valuable assets an attacker can target — and regulators expect demonstrable security controls, not just good intentions.

Quatech's FinSec practice applies our core cybersecurity capability — penetration testing, vulnerability assessment, threat intelligence, and security tool development — to the specific context of financial services: PCI DSS cardholder environments, FCA-regulated systems, Open Banking APIs, and payment processing infrastructure.

We combine technical security capability with regulatory awareness so our findings are framed in terms that your compliance team, board, and regulators can act on — not just your IT team.

Threat modelling for financial platforms and payment systems

Penetration testing of financial web applications and APIs

PCI DSS security control testing and gap assessment

Open Banking API security review

Social engineering and phishing simulations for financial staff

Board-level reporting with regulatory context included

Service Process

01

Scoping & Rules of Engagement

We document what systems are in scope, what testing techniques are authorised, who is informed, and what the regulatory context is. All testing is explicitly authorised in writing before commencement.

02

Threat Modelling

We model the realistic threat landscape for your organisation — which threat actors are motivated to target you, what their objectives are, and which attack paths are most likely given your profile.

03

Technical Testing

Authorised penetration testing and vulnerability assessment of in-scope systems using PTES methodology and OWASP testing guides, with financial-sector-specific test cases included.

04

Staff Awareness Testing

Where authorised: phishing simulation, vishing tests, and USB drop exercises targeting financial sector staff who handle payments, customer data, or system access.

05

Findings & Reporting

Technical findings report with CVSS v3.1 severity ratings, proof-of-concept evidence, and remediation guidance. Executive summary written for board and regulator audience, not just technical teams.

06

Remediation Verification

After remediation, identified vulnerabilities are re-tested to confirm fixes are effective. Updated report issued for audit evidence and regulatory reporting.
