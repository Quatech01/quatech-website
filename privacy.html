Privacy Policy

Effective date: 4 September 2026  ·  Quatech  ·  quatech.co.uk

1. Who We Are

Quatech is a UK-based cybersecurity and software engineering company. We build websites, mobile apps, and security tools for businesses and individuals. Our website is quatech.co.uk and you can contact us at info@quatech.co.uk.

2. Information We Collect

We collect information you provide directly, such as:

  • Name and email address when you submit a contact or enquiry form
  • Message content from enquiry forms
  • Any other information you choose to provide

We do not use tracking cookies, analytics scripts, or advertising networks on this website.

3. How We Use Your Information

Information submitted via our contact forms is used solely to:

  • Respond to your enquiry
  • Provide the services you have requested
  • Send service-related communications

We do not sell, rent, or share your personal data with third parties for marketing purposes.

4. Our Apps & Tools

Quatech publishes standalone apps and security tools. Each has its own data practices:

Q-Authenticator (Android/iOS)

All data stored locally on your device, encrypted with your PIN. No data is collected or transmitted. Camera used only for QR scanning. Full policy: quatech.co.uk/privacy-q-authenticator.html

Cyberlab Security Tools (essentials, gdpr, csrb, sbom, steg, mfa subdomains)

These tools process data you submit locally in your browser or on our servers solely to generate the requested security report. No data is retained after your session ends.

5. Data Retention

Enquiry form submissions are retained only as long as necessary to fulfil your request, typically no longer than 12 months. You may request deletion at any time by emailing info@quatech.co.uk.

6. Your Rights (UK GDPR)

Under UK GDPR you have the right to:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you
  • Request correction of inaccurate data
  • Request deletion of your data
  • Object to processing of your data
  • Lodge a complaint with the ICO at ico.org.uk

7. Security

We take reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect your data. All data transmitted to our services is encrypted in transit via HTTPS.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Material changes will be reflected in the effective date above. Continued use of our services after changes constitutes acceptance.
