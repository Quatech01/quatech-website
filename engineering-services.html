Engineering Services / Design

CAD/CAM Design Services

From concept sketches to fully machined components — our CAD/CAM team delivers precision-engineered designs ready for production.

About This Service

Quatech's CAD/CAM design team provides comprehensive 2D drafting, 3D solid modelling, and CNC machining programming services for engineering components across the oil & gas, manufacturing, power, and construction industries.

We work with industry-standard software platforms including AutoCAD, SolidWorks, CATIA, and Mastercam to deliver designs that meet client specifications and international standards such as ASME, BS, and ISO.

All designs are reviewed by our senior engineers before release to ensure dimensional accuracy, manufacturing feasibility, and compliance with applicable codes.

2D/3D component modelling and detailed engineering drawings

CNC tool path programming and simulation

Reverse engineering of legacy components

Design for manufacturability (DFM) reviews

FEA stress analysis and optimisation

As-built drawing production

Service Procedures

01

Client Brief & Scope Definition

We begin with a detailed client consultation to understand functional requirements, operating environment, material preferences, and dimensional constraints.

02

Conceptual Design & Layout

Our engineers produce initial concept drawings and 3D layouts, presented to the client for review and approval before detailed design commences.

03

Detailed 3D Modelling

Full parametric 3D models are created in SolidWorks or CATIA, incorporating GD&T tolerancing and material specifications.

04

Drawing Package Production

Complete 2D engineering drawing packages are produced, including assembly drawings, part drawings, bill of materials, and revision history.

05

CAM Programming & Simulation

CNC machining programs are generated in Mastercam and simulated to verify toolpaths, cycle times, and surface finish requirements before shop floor release.

06

Design Review & Handover

Final drawings and CAM files are reviewed by a senior engineer, issued with a design certificate, and handed over in agreed formats (DXF, STEP, PDF, NC code).

Engineering Services / Materials

Steel Chemical Composition & Mechanical Properties Testing

Accurate, accredited laboratory testing to verify the chemical and mechanical integrity of steel and metallic materials.

About This Service

Quatech provides comprehensive metallurgical testing services for steel and other metallic materials, verifying that chemical composition and mechanical properties conform to purchase specifications, material standards, and regulatory requirements.

Our testing is performed by qualified metallurgists using calibrated, accredited equipment. We issue detailed test reports with full traceability, supporting materials receiving inspection, quality assurance, and failure investigation programmes.

We test to international standards including ASTM, BS EN, ISO, and API specifications across carbon steel, alloy steel, stainless steel, and duplex grades.

Optical emission spectrometry (OES) for chemical composition

Tensile, yield, and elongation testing

Charpy impact testing (CVN) at ambient and sub-zero temperatures

Hardness testing (Brinell, Vickers, Rockwell)

Microstructural examination and grain size analysis

Full test reports with material traceability

Service Procedures

01

Sample Receipt & Registration

Material samples are received, logged, and uniquely identified. Chain of custody is maintained throughout testing.

02

Chemical Composition Analysis

OES arc spark testing determines the elemental composition of the steel, verifying conformance to specified grade chemistry (e.g. C, Si, Mn, P, S, Cr, Ni, Mo).

03

Mechanical Test Specimen Preparation

Test specimens are machined to standard geometries in accordance with ASTM E8 (tensile), ASTM E23 (Charpy), and applicable hardness standards.

04

Mechanical Testing

Specimens are tested on calibrated universal testing machines and impact testing equipment. All test data is recorded digitally with load-extension curves retained.

05

Microstructural Examination

Metallographic sections are prepared, etched, and examined under optical microscopy to assess microstructure, grain size, inclusions, and phase constituents.

06

Test Report Issuance

A comprehensive test report is issued, including all test results, specimen details, applicable standards, acceptance criteria, and a signed certificate of conformance.

Engineering Services / Protection

Corrosion Control Services

Protecting assets, extending operational life, and ensuring structural integrity through expert corrosion assessment and mitigation strategies.

About This Service

Quatech's corrosion engineering team provides assessment, prevention, and remediation services for industrial plant, pipelines, offshore structures, and civil infrastructure. Our approach combines industry best practice with advanced diagnostic techniques to identify corrosion risk early and implement effective, cost-efficient solutions.

We operate across the oil & gas, petrochemical, water, marine, and construction sectors, working to NACE/AMPP, ISO, and client-specific standards. Our services cover both internal and external corrosion threats.

From initial corrosion surveys to the design and implementation of cathodic protection systems and protective coating schemes, Quatech provides a full-service corrosion management capability.

Corrosion risk assessment and condition surveys

Cathodic protection (CP) design and installation

Protective coating specification and inspection

Corrosion inhibitor selection and monitoring

Internal corrosion monitoring for pipelines

Fitness-for-service (FFS) assessments

Service Procedures

01

Initial Corrosion Survey

Visual inspection, ultrasonic wall thickness measurements, and data collection to establish baseline corrosion condition and identify areas of concern.

02

Corrosion Risk Assessment

Identification of corrosion mechanisms (e.g. galvanic, crevice, stress corrosion cracking, MIC), severity ranking, and consequence analysis in accordance with NACE SP0169 and API 581.

03

Mitigation Strategy Development

A tailored corrosion management plan is developed, recommending cathodic protection, coating systems, inhibitor treatment, material upgrades, or a combination of measures.

04

Implementation & Installation

Installation of CP systems (sacrificial anodes or impressed current), application of specified coatings by trained operatives, and commissioning of monitoring systems.

05

Monitoring & Inspection

Periodic CP potential surveys, coating condition inspections, corrosion coupon analysis, and corrosion rate monitoring to verify mitigation effectiveness.

06

Reporting & Recommendations

Comprehensive inspection and monitoring reports are issued, including corrosion data trends, remaining life assessments, and recommended follow-up actions.

Engineering Services / Inspection

Welding & Non-Destructive Testing (NDT)

Certified welding inspection and advanced NDT services ensuring structural integrity, weld quality, and compliance with international codes.

About This Service

Quatech's welding and NDT division provides independent inspection and testing services for welds, fabrications, and in-service structures across the oil & gas, power generation, petrochemical, and construction industries.

Our certified inspectors and NDT technicians apply the full range of NDT methods — including Ultrasonic Testing (UT), Radiographic Testing (RT), Magnetic Particle Inspection (MPI/MT), and Dye Penetrant Testing (PT) — to detect surface and sub-surface discontinuities without damaging the component.

We operate to ASME, AWS, BS EN ISO, and API welding and NDT standards, with all personnel qualified to CSWIP, PCN, or equivalent internationally recognised schemes.

Welding Procedure Specification (WPS) and PQR review and development

Welder qualification testing (WQT/WPQ)

Ultrasonic Testing (UT) including TOFD and Phased Array (PAUT)

Radiographic Testing (RT) — conventional and digital

Magnetic Particle Inspection (MPI) and Dye Penetrant Testing (PT)

Visual inspection (VT) and dimensional verification

Service Procedures

01

Scope & Standards Review

Client welding drawings, specifications, and applicable codes (ASME IX, AWS D1.1, BS EN ISO 15614) are reviewed to define the inspection and testing programme.

02

Pre-Weld Inspection

Base material verification, joint fit-up, weld preparation geometry, and preheat checks are carried out prior to welding commencement.

03

In-Process Welding Inspection

In-process monitoring of welding parameters, interpass temperatures, consumable verification, and visual examination of completed weld passes.

04

Post-Weld Visual Inspection (VT)

100% visual examination of completed welds to detect surface imperfections including cracks, undercut, porosity, and profile defects to acceptance criteria.

05

NDT Examination

Volumetric (UT/RT) and surface (MPI/PT) NDT is applied per the agreed inspection plan. PAUT/TOFD is offered for complex geometries and high-integrity applications.

06

Reporting & Certification

All inspection and NDT results are documented in formal inspection reports, NDT reports, and weld maps. Certificates of conformance are issued upon satisfactory completion.
