Quatech Technology Scholarship

Annual financial award for UK students from underrepresented backgrounds pursuing degrees in Computer Science, Cybersecurity, or Software Engineering. Priority given to first-generation university students.

Open to undergraduate and postgraduate students at UK universities
Merit and means-tested — both academic promise and financial need considered
Recipients also gain access to mentorship and Quatech's lab resources

1-to-1 Mentorship Programme

Free structured mentorship pairing early-career professionals and students with Quatech engineers. Sessions cover career navigation, technical growth, breaking into cybersecurity, and building a portfolio.

Monthly 1-hour sessions over a 6-month engagement
Focus areas: cybersecurity, full-stack development, career strategy
Open to those without industry connections or professional networks

How the Scholarship Works

A straightforward, fair process. No hidden criteria, no industry connections required.

01

Apply Online

Submit a short application form sharing your background, course, financial situation, and what you hope to achieve in technology.

02

Shortlisting

Applications are reviewed by the Quatech team against both academic merit and financial need. We contact shortlisted candidates within four weeks.

03

Conversation

A short informal call to understand your goals and answer any questions. No formal interview — just a conversation.

04

Award & Support

Recipients receive the scholarship award plus ongoing mentorship access and inclusion in the Quatech alumni network for future opportunities.
